# Privacy Policy for Actuarial Exam App 
**Last Updated:** April 15, 2025  

Thank you for using **Actuarial Exam App**. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our actuarial exam preparation app.  

## 1. Information We Collect  
The app collects:  
- **User Responses:** Your answers to practice questions.  
- **Attempt Timestamps:** Date/time of each question attempt.  
- **Performance Data:** Aggregated statistics (e.g., correct/incorrect rates).
- **Email Address:** Your email address is used to create an account for tracking your progress across multiple devices.  

**We do NOT collect:**  
- Personal identifiers (name, age, etc.).  
- Location or device identifiers.  
- Payment/sensitive data.  

## 2. How We Use Your Data  
- To personalize your learning experience.  
- To improve app functionality and content.  
- For anonymized analytics (e.g., common mistakes).  

## 3. Data Storage & Security  
- Data is stored locally on your device or in anonymized form on secure servers (if cloud sync is enabled).  
- No third parties access your raw response data.  

## 4. Third-Party Services  
We use:  
- **Google Firebase Analytics:** For aggregated usage statistics (see [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).  

## 5. User Rights  
- **Delete Data:** Clear app cache or use the in-app reset option.  
- **Opt Out:** Disable analytics in app settings (if applicable).  

## 6. Policy Updates  
Changes will be posted here. Continued use implies acceptance.
